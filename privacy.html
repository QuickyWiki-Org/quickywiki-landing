Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

QuickyWiki ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through our website at quickywiki.app and how we use it. The QuickyWiki mobile app is not yet released; this policy will be updated before launch to cover the app itself.

Information we collect

  • Waitlist email address. When you join our waitlist, we collect the email address you provide. That's it — no name, no phone number, no tracking profile.
  • Basic technical data. Like most websites, our hosting infrastructure may log standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, used solely to keep the site running and secure.

How we use your information

  • To notify you when QuickyWiki launches and share occasional updates about the product.
  • To operate, maintain, and protect our website.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Where your data lives

Waitlist emails are stored securely on our backend infrastructure hosted with Microsoft Azure. Access is limited to the QuickyWiki team.

How long we keep it

We keep your waitlist email until QuickyWiki launches and for a reasonable period afterwards, or until you ask us to remove it — whichever comes first.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. Just email us and we'll take care of it promptly.

Cookies

Our website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Children

Our website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time — for example, when the QuickyWiki app launches. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the latest version.

Contact us

Questions or requests? Email us at quickywiki.support@gmail.com or visit our contact page.
